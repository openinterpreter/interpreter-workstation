# sharp-libvips v1.3.4 third-party notices

The `@img/sharp-libvips-*` v1.3.4 platform packages contain precompiled,
dynamically loaded, replaceable shared libraries. Their package metadata
declares `LGPL-3.0-or-later`. The exact packaging/build source and archive are:

- https://github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips/tree/v1.3.4
- https://github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips/archive/refs/tags/v1.3.4.tar.gz

The [upstream component notices for this exact release](https://github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips/blob/v1.3.4/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md)
identify the licenses of its included libraries (including cairo under MPL
1.1 and the LGPL-covered components). The LGPL-3.0 text and incorporated
GPL-3.0 text are distributed alongside this notice. Users may replace the
shared libraries and reverse-engineer them to debug modifications.
